# Week 8 — Learning Objectives

By the end of this capstone module, students will be able to:

## Knowledge (Remember / Understand)

1. Summarize how weeks 1–7 practices combine into a **production-grade Terraform delivery system**.
2. Compare the four capstone tracks and articulate tradeoffs for a given organizational scenario.
3. Explain capstone evaluation criteria: architecture, Terraform quality, CI/CD, security, documentation, demo.

## Skills (Apply / Create)

4. Implement a capstone project meeting deliverables in [`../../capstone/README.md`](../../capstone/README.md).
5. Operate remote state, modules, environment promotion, drift awareness, recovery runbooks, and security validation in one solution.
6. Deliver a 15–20 minute presentation covering business context, architecture, live or recorded CI demo, security, and cost.
7. Produce supporting artifacts: diagrams, security review, cost estimate, and operations notes (`make lab-stop`, cleanup).

## Professional practice (Evaluate)

8. Peer-review another team’s capstone against the rubric and provide constructive, criteria-based feedback.
9. Reflect on lessons learned and a realistic “next 90 days” roadmap for their organization or portfolio narrative.

## Bloom’s alignment

| Level | Objective # |
|-------|----------------|
| Understand | 1–3 |
| Create | 4–7 |
| Evaluate | 8–9 |

## Course grade alignment

Capstone contributes **30%** of overall course grade per [`../../capstone/README.md`](../../capstone/README.md) rubric (Architecture, Terraform quality, CI/CD & ops, Security, Docs & demo).
