### Year-End Narrative — Influence Senior Stakeholders & Act as a Trusted Advisor

Throughout the year, I strengthened my role as a **trusted architectural advisor to technical, product, and leadership stakeholders**, helping teams navigate complex and sometimes ambiguous architectural decisions. My focus was not only on recommending technical solutions, but on clearly framing **trade-offs, risks, cost, scalability, resiliency, operational impact, time-to-value, and long-term strategic implications** so stakeholders could make informed decisions.

Within the Enterprise File Transfer initiative, I led and contributed to architecture discussions involving significant design choices, including **multi-region Active-Active resiliency, data consistency, orchestration and compute patterns, scalability, and operational complexity**. I evaluated alternatives such as DynamoDB versus Aurora and Step Functions versus Fargate, helping stakeholders understand both immediate implementation considerations and longer-term platform consequences.

A significant outcome was progressing the target-state architecture through enterprise governance and achieving **conditional ARC approval**. This required communicating recommendations clearly, responding to architectural concerns, incorporating feedback, and building alignment across architecture, engineering, product, and leadership stakeholders. I also continued serving as a technical and architectural escalation point for the team as the backend engine moved through implementation, knowledge transfer, higher environments, and production-readiness activities.

Overall, I made strong progress in moving from providing technical recommendations to **influencing architectural direction and enabling better strategic decisions**. Going forward, I will continue expanding this trusted-advisor role across broader architecture forums and initiatives, with increased emphasis on executive-level communication, enterprise-wide trade-offs, and long-term technology strategy.
