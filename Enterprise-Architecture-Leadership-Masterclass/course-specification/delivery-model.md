# Delivery Model

## Duration

10 weeks

## Weekly commitment

| Activity | Time |
| -------- | ---- |
| Live class | 2 hours |
| Optional office hour | 1 hour |
| Assignment and lab work | 3–5 hours |
| Architecture artifact | 1 per week |

**Estimated total effort:** ~60 hours

---

## Recommended live-session structure (2 hours)

```text
Part 1: Business scenario and lesson        ~15 min
Part 2: Architecture concept                ~20 min
Part 3: Instructor demonstration            ~15 min
Part 4: Guided lab                          ~40 min
Part 5: Architecture review                 ~15 min
Part 6: Assignment briefing                 ~10 min
Buffer / breaks                             ~5 min
```

Instructors may adjust timing for corporate workshops while preserving all six parts.

---

## Cohort model

| Mode | Size | Notes |
| ---- | ---- | ----- |
| Open BayLearn cohort | 8–15 | Peer review and ARB role-play work best in this range |
| Corporate private cohort | 10–25 | May use breakout ARBs; keep facilitation guides |

---

## Student completion requirements

Students must:

1. Attend at least **70%** of live sessions
2. Complete at least **80%** of required assignments
3. Achieve an overall score of at least **70%**
4. Submit the final capstone
5. Present the capstone to the review panel

---

## Office hours

Optional 1-hour weekly sessions for:

- Lab blockers (especially AWS labs)
- Artifact feedback
- Capstone narrative coaching
- Career / portfolio questions

---

## Tools students may use

| Purpose | Examples |
| ------- | -------- |
| Diagramming | Lucidchart, Draw.io, Miro, Figma, Mermaid |
| Documents | Markdown, Google Docs, Word |
| Spreadsheets | Excel, Google Sheets, CSV |
| Slides | PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote |
| Cloud labs | AWS account (student or BayLearn lab account) |
| IaC | Terraform (Modules 5–8) |

BayLearn does not require a single diagramming vendor; deliverables must be readable and exportable.

---

## Instructor preparation (per module)

- Review instructor guide and speaking script
- Prepare whiteboard sequence
- Validate lab environment / Terraform (AWS modules)
- Review reference solution privately
- Confirm grading guide and common misconceptions

---

## Corporate delivery adaptations

- Compress Modules 1–2 into a kickoff workshop if needed
- Keep NorthStar as the shared case study unless a custom private case study is contracted separately
- Emphasize ARB simulation and executive memo formats for leadership audiences
- AWS labs may be demonstration-led for non-hands-on cohorts, with take-home optional

---

## Future self-paced conversion notes

Materials are structured so live segments can become:

- Recorded concept videos (from speaking scripts)
- Self-guided labs (from student instructions)
- Asynchronous peer review (from ARB checklists)
- Timed quizzes (from existing quiz banks)

Self-paced packaging is out of scope for initial live launch but must remain feasible.
