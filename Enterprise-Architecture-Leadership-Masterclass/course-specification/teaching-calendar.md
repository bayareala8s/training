# 10-Week Teaching Calendar

**Course:** Enterprise Architecture Leadership Masterclass  
**Default:** One live session per week (120 min) + optional office hour (60 min)

Customize dates in BayLearn `cohort.json` / your calendar. Below is the instructional sequence.

| Week | Live focus | Primary artifact due | Office-hour emphasis |
| ---: | ---------- | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| 1 | EA role, operating model, principles | Architecture mission, RACI, principles, risk register | Role clarity vs SA/EM |
| 2 | Capability mapping & value streams | L1/L2 map, heatmap, stakeholder matrix, KPI traceability | Capability vs process confusion |
| 3 | Current-state & TIME portfolio | Heatmap, integration sketch, top-10 risks, exec findings | Inventory CSV scoring |
| 4 | Target state & 24-month roadmap | Target architecture, 3 transitions, roadmap, exec summary | Sequencing & coexistence |
| 5 | Cloud & platform + Lab 5 deploy | Cloud strategy, LZ diagram, FinOps policy, ADR, TF evidence | Cleanup & budgets |
| 6 | Integration/data + Lab 6 | Reference architecture, pattern matrix, ADRs, cost note | Pattern selection |
| 7 | Security/resilience + Lab 7 | Threat model, RTO/RPO, DR runbook, control matrix | Proportionate controls |
| 8 | AI strategy + Lab 8 | Use-case portfolio, AI RA, eval results, governance checklist | HITL & cost |
| 9 | ARB simulation | Review pack, decision memo, ADRs, 5-slide briefing | Role-play facilitation |
| 10 | Capstone presentations | Full capstone pack + 15-slide deck | Dry runs / Q&A |
