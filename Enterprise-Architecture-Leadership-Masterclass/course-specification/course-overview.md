## Core promise

Students will learn how to connect business strategy to technology direction, evaluate complex trade-offs, design enterprise target states, govern architecture decisions, and communicate effectively with executives and engineering teams.

---

## Positioning

### This course is

A practical architecture leadership program where students work through a realistic enterprise transformation and produce professional architecture artifacts suitable for portfolios, corporate delivery, and BayLearn live cohorts.

### This course is not

- A TOGAF exam-preparation course
- A basic AWS architecture class
- A collection of cloud-service tutorials
- A generic system-design interview course
- A passive video library

---

## Curriculum at a glance

1. The Enterprise Architect’s Role  
2. Business Architecture and Capability Mapping  
3. Current-State Architecture Assessment  
4. Target-State Architecture and Transformation Roadmaps  
5. Cloud and Platform Strategy  
6. Integration, Application, and Data Architecture  
7. Security, Risk, Compliance, and Resilience  
8. AI Strategy and Intelligent Enterprise Architecture  
9. Architecture Governance and Executive Communication  
10. Capstone and Architecture Leadership  

---

## Capstone

**NorthStar Enterprise Transformation Program**

Students integrate weekly artifacts into a complete enterprise transformation proposal and defend it in a formal architecture review (15-minute presentation + 10-minute Q&A).
