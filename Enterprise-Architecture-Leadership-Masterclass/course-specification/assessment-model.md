# Assessment Model

## Grade weights

| Assessment | Weight |
| ---------- | -----: |
| Weekly labs | 35% |
| Architecture artifacts | 20% |
| Architecture review participation | 10% |
| Capstone document | 20% |
| Final presentation | 15% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

---

## Completion thresholds

| Requirement | Threshold |
| ----------- | --------- |
| Live session attendance | ≥ 70% |
| Required assignments completed | ≥ 80% |
| Overall course score | ≥ 70% |
| Capstone document | Submitted |
| Capstone presentation | Delivered to panel |

---

## Standard architecture rubric

Each architecture submission is graded on:

| Criterion | Weight |
| --------- | -----: |
| Business alignment | 20% |
| Architecture quality | 20% |
| Trade-off analysis | 20% |
| Security and resilience | 15% |
| Feasibility and roadmap | 15% |
| Communication quality | 10% |

### Scoring levels

| Score | Label | Meaning |
| ----- | ----- | ------- |
| 4 | Excellent | Executive-ready; clear trade-offs; strong feasibility; minimal revision needed |
| 3 | Proficient | Meets expectations; minor gaps; suitable for cohort standard |
| 2 | Developing | Partial; material gaps in analysis, feasibility, or communication |
| 1 | Incomplete | Missing critical elements or not usable as an architecture artifact |

Detailed level descriptors: [`../assessments/rubrics/standard-architecture-rubric.md`](../assessments/rubrics/standard-architecture-rubric.md)

---

## Weekly labs (35%)

- One primary lab per module
- Graded with the standard rubric (module-specific notes may adjust emphasis)
- Late policy: defined per cohort in BayLearn cohort settings (default: 10% deduction per day, max 3 days)

## Architecture artifacts (20%)

- Weekly professional artifacts feeding the capstone
- Quality and consistency across the portfolio matter as much as individual scores

## Architecture review participation (10%)

- Especially Module 9 ARB simulation and peer reviews
- Criteria: preparation, role fidelity, constructive challenge, decision quality

## Capstone document (20%)

- Integrates required artifacts (see capstone brief)
- Graded with the standard rubric plus completeness checklist

## Final presentation (15%)

- 15 minutes + 10 minutes Q&A
- Maximum 15 slides
- Criteria: narrative clarity, decision requests, trade-off defense, executive language

---

## Quizzes

Quizzes are **formative by default** (learning checks). Cohorts may optionally weight quizzes at instructor discretion; if weighted, total weights must be re-normalized and communicated before Week 1.

Each module quiz includes:

- 10 multiple-choice questions
- 3 scenario questions
- 2 discussion questions
- Answer explanations
- Difficulty tags
- Learning-objective mapping

Categories: Recall, Application, Trade-off analysis, Architecture judgment, Leadership judgment

---

## Academic integrity

- Collaboration is encouraged on understanding; submitted artifacts must be the student’s own work
- NorthStar datasets and templates may be shared; answers and reference solutions must not be circulated among students
- AI assistants may be used as drafting aids if disclosed per cohort policy; students remain accountable for architecture judgment and originality
