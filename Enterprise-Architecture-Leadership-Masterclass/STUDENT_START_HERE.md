# Student Start Here

**Course:** Enterprise Architecture Leadership Masterclass  
**Tagline:** From Senior Engineer to Enterprise Architecture Leader  
**Platform:** BayLearn

Welcome. You are the newly appointed **Lead Enterprise Architect** for **NorthStar Financial Services** — a fictional financial services enterprise used throughout this cohort.

---

## What you will produce

Each week you create one professional architecture artifact. By Week 10 you assemble them into the **NorthStar Enterprise Transformation Program** capstone and present to a review panel.

---

## Before Week 1

1. Read [`course-specification/northstar-case-study.md`](course-specification/northstar-case-study.md)
2. Skim [`course-specification/learning-outcomes.md`](course-specification/learning-outcomes.md)
3. Open your workbook: [`student/workbook/README.md`](student/workbook/README.md)
4. Bookmark templates: [`student/templates/README.md`](student/templates/README.md)
5. Complete the Week 1 self-assessment in the workbook

## Weekly rhythm

| Activity | Time |
| -------- | ---- |
| Live class | 2 hours |
| Optional office hour | 1 hour |
| Lab + assignment | 3–5 hours |

Live sessions follow: scenario → concept → demo → lab → review → assignment briefing.

---

## Completion requirements

- Attend ≥ 70% of live sessions  
- Complete ≥ 80% of required assignments  
- Overall score ≥ 70%  
- Submit and present the capstone  

Grading: [`course-specification/assessment-model.md`](course-specification/assessment-model.md)
