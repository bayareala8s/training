# Discussion Questions — Module 04

**Module:** Target-State Architecture and Transformation Roadmaps  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Use:** Live discussion, breakouts, office hours

---

## Concept checks

1. How does a target-state architecture differ from a business vision statement?  
2. Why are non-goals essential in target-state definition?  
3. How do the seven modernization strategies relate to—but differ from—TIME?  
4. What makes a transition architecture different from a project phase gate?

## Trade-off discussions

5. For StarCore, defend retain+replatform against a rewrite proposal from a vendor.  
6. When would you replace both CRMs instead of consolidating to a survivor?  
7. Is rehosting a fleet of batch apps a win or a deferred failure for NorthStar?  
8. How long should dual-write of customer golden record be allowed to run?

## Leadership discussions

9. Elena Vos resists retiring LegacyCRM. What influence approaches do you use?  
10. Raj Patel wants risk-first sequencing; Maya wants CX wins by Month 9. How do you design a coalition roadmap?  
11. A BU asks to extend FileBridge “temporary” for 18 months. What do you recommend to ARB?

## Scenario prompts (breakout)

12. **Funding cut:** CFO removes 30% of transformation budget. Renegotiate outcomes and sequence.  
13. **Incident:** Major payments outage during PayForge replatform. Do you pause roadmap waves? What changes?  
14. **Shadow AI:** A team launches an unsanctioned LLM tool during Phase 1. How does your target principles response work?

## Capstone link

15. Which decisions in Module 04 must remain stable into the capstone, and which should stay intentionally open until Modules 05–07?
