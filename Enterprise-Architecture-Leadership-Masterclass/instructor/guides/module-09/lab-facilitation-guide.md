# Lab Facilitation Guide — Module 09 ARB

## Setup (before class)

- Distribute proposal pack at least 24 hours prior when possible  
- Pre-assign roles; publish in BayLearn announcement  
- Print disposition scorecards or share collaborative doc  
- Decide who plays Proposer (instructor recommended for consistency)

## During simulation

1. Enforce timeboxes ruthlessly—especially the proposer walkthrough.  
2. Interrupt aesthetic debates; redirect to risk, alternatives, conditions.  
3. If the room moves to full Approve, inject the CIO question: “What precedent did we just set for Wealth and Banking BUs?”  
4. Require split dispositions even if conversation blends topics.

## Mid-lab check prompts

- “Name your hardest decision request.”  
- “What evidence would flip your disposition?”  
- “Who owns each condition?”

## After simulation

- Spot-share one strong challenge question and one weak “concern.”  
- Show the shape of the reference memo (not full answer) on screen if needed.  
- Remind: individual submissions even if board was collective.

## Remote cohort tips

- Use breakout rooms by role pairs for 8 minutes, then plenary disposition.  
- Capture scorecard in a shared spreadsheet.
