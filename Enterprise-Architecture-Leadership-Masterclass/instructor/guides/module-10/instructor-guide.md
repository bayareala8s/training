# Instructor Guide — Module 10: Capstone and Architecture Leadership

## 1. Module purpose

Convert ten weeks of work into an executive-ready transformation proposal and a practiced defense. Confirm panel logistics and grading readiness.

## 2. Learning objectives

M10-LO1 through M10-LO4.

## 3. Prerequisites

Confirm students have access to capstone brief and their artifact history. Schedule review panels.

## 4. Estimated timing (120 minutes)

| Segment | Minutes |
| ------- | ------: |
| Narrative craft | 20 |
| Trade-off defense practice | 25 |
| Presentation craft + dry run tips | 20 |
| Guided finalization lab | 30 |
| Mini dry-run / peer challenge | 15 |
| Assignment & panel logistics | 10 |

*Note: Actual graded presentations may be scheduled in extended sessions or breakouts beyond the 120-minute block.*

## 5. Opening business scenario

“The committee will not read your repo. They will hear a story and ask for decisions.”

## 6. Lesson flow

1. Narrative arc  
2. SAY defense pattern with live drills  
3. Slide budget and timing  
4. Portfolio/career plan  
5. Finalization lab  
6. Panel rules and grading

## 7. Questions to ask

1. What is your one-sentence program promise?  
2. What will you cut at minute 12?  
3. What challenge are you afraid of?

## 8. Whiteboard sequence

See `whiteboard-plan.md`.

## 9. Demonstration steps

1. Show a 3-minute “good” narrative spine.  
2. Model SAY on a cloud vs. multi-cloud challenge.  
3. Show a bad slide (walls of text) vs. one-idea slide.

## 10. Break points

After defense drills; before finalization lab.

## 11. Lab facilitation

Help students triage gaps: completeness first, then story, then polish.

## 12. Common student issues

| Issue | Response |
| ----- | -------- |
| Artifact hoarding in deck | Move to appendix; keep spine |
| Contradictions across weeks | Reconciliation workshop |
| Panic over missing M5–8 AWS evidence | Accept architecture narrative with honest gaps |

## 13. Debrief questions

See module `debrief-questions.md`.

## 14. Assignment briefing

Point to Module 10 assignment (capstone submission + leadership plan).

## 15. Suggested homework

- Final rehearsal  
- Completeness remediation  
- Leadership plan
