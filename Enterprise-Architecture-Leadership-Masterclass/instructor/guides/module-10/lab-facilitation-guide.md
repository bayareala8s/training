# Lab Facilitation — Module 10

## Triage order for office hours

1. Completeness checklist gaps  
2. Contradictory recommendations  
3. Missing asks  
4. Timing / slide count  
5. Visual polish (last)

## Dry-run protocol

- 5-minute lightning version for peer feedback if full 15s won’t fit  
- One challenge question per student minimum  
- Timer visible

## Panel day

- Brief panelists on fiction notice and rubric  
- No grading debates in front of students  
- Capture scores independently then moderate
