# Discussion Questions — Module 01: The Enterprise Architect’s Role

**Use:** Live facilitation, cold-calls, and office hours  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Rule:** No single scripted “correct” answer; grade reasoning and trade-offs

---

## Opening (scenario)

1. If NorthStar’s architecture function vanished tomorrow, which leadership intent degrades first—and through what mechanism?  
2. Why did three overlapping identity projects get funded—what system failure does that reveal beyond “bad architects”?

---

## Lesson 1.1 — What EA is

3. Where does solution architecture end and enterprise architecture begin on a partner-onboarding modernization?  
4. What metrics would convince a skeptical CFO that EA is not overhead?  
5. When is “EA as embedded senior designer” justified—and how do you prevent it from becoming the operating model?

---

## Lesson 1.2 — Operating model

6. What is the minimum set of Year-1 enterprise-accountable decisions at NorthStar?  
7. If a BU president says architecture slows delivery, what do you change first—and what do you refuse to dilute?  
8. Guardrails vs. gates: where should NorthStar invest more in the next two quarters?  
9. Should the Lead EA report to CIO, CTO, or a transformation office? Trade-offs?

---

## Lesson 1.3 — Principles

10. Which principle most threatens local autonomy—and how do you socialize it without a pure power play?  
11. Is “secure by design” one principle or an implication on every principle?  
12. How many principles is too many for NorthStar—and what goes into standards instead?

---

## Lesson 1.4 — Leadership

13. Name an influence source you over-rely on and one you underuse.  
14. Wealth announces a CRM platform in a town hall. What is your first 48-hour plan?  
15. If readiness scores are mostly 2s, what is the smallest viable governance increment you still defend to the CIO?

---

## Lab / debrief cross-overs

16. Stress-test a second API gateway through your RACI—where does accountability sit?  
17. Which architecture-function risk is most likely in the next 90 days?  
18. Complete: “Architecture leadership at NorthStar succeeds when ___ without needing ___.”
