# Whiteboard Plan — Module 02

**Time:** ~15–20 minutes across session  
**Materials:** Digital whiteboard or physical board + sticky notes

---

## Board 1 — Strategy bridge (5 min)

Columns: **Theme | KPI | Capability question | Constraint**

Seed with Cost, CX, Speed, Resilience. Leave a blank row for student input.

## Board 2 — Capability clusters (8 min)

Six domains as swim lanes: Customer · Payments · Partner · Risk/Trust · Platforms · Corporate

Students shout L1 candidates; instructor places only org/system-independent names. Park rejects in a “Not capabilities” column.

## Board 3 — Onboarding value stream (7 min)

Horizontal stages: Capture → Identity → KYC → Product setup → Activation → Monitoring

Vertical sticky notes: capabilities invoked (blue) and frictions (red). Circle top two frictions for investment themes.

## Facilitation tips

- Ban application names on Boards 1–2.
- When debate stalls, ask for a 60-second “disagree and commit” and document the assumption.
- Photograph boards for cohort share-out (no student PII).
