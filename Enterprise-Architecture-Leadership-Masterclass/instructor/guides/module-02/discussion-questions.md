2. **Ownership:** Should Customer Data Management be owned by a CDO, jointly by LOBs, or by a federated model? What fails in each option?

4. **Heatmap ethics:** A director wants Identity scored green before an audit. How do you respond as Lead EA?
5. **Scope:** Why might publishing a Level 3 map in Week 2 harm transformation credibility?
6. **Cross-stream effects:** How does Incident Response value-stream weakness show up as customer onboarding risk?
