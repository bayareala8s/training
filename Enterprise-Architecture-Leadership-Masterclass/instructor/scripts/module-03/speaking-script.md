# Speaking Script — Module 03: Current-State Architecture Assessment

**Total runtime target:** 120 minutes  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## [0:00–0:05] Welcome and framing

> Welcome back. Module 03: Current-State Architecture Assessment.  
> You remain Lead Enterprise Architect at NorthStar Financial Services—fictional.  
> By the end of this session you will scope discovery, apply TIME with rationale, call out dependency hubs, and brief a top-risk narrative executives can fund.

---

## [0:05–0:15] Business scenario

> Three hundred-plus applications, unclear ownership, ExCo wants risk and cost levers this quarter. A six-month CMDB cleanse is not a strategy. Discovery is a design choice.

**Ask:** What decision are we trying to unlock in the next thirty days?

---

## [0:15–0:35] Architecture concept

> We move from decision to scope to evidence to confidence. Then TIME: Tolerate, Invest, Migrate, Eliminate. Dispositions without dimensions are opinions. Dimensions without rationale are false precision.

**Whiteboard:** TIME columns; place two partner gateways.

**CFU:** When is Tolerate the right leadership answer?

---

## [0:35–0:50] Instructor demonstration

> I will filter the fictional inventory, score a few apps, and deliberately overturn at least one CSV seed disposition. Then I will mark the ESB hub and dual customer masters—and say what that does to sequencing.

1. Filter by criticality/capability.  
2. Score and overturn one seed.  
3. Sketch hub/duplicate implications.

---

## [0:50–1:30] Guided lab

> Forty minutes. Scope, TIME, dependencies, top risks. Individual submissions. Mid-lab check at 1:10: Which disposition is hardest—and what dependency makes it hard?

---

## [1:30–1:45] Architecture review

> Volunteer artifact. ARB questions: alignment to strategy, residual risk, alternatives, feasibility of Eliminate dates.

---

## [1:45–1:55] Assignment briefing

> Executive current-state briefing for ExCo pre-read. Capstone: TIME summary + risk register feed roadmap. Rubric: alignment, trade-offs, feasibility.

---

## [1:55–2:00] Close

> Takeaways: scope discovery; TIME is judgment; dependencies sequence change; debt needs business interest language. Next: Target-state and roadmaps.

---

## Optional office-hour prompts

- Calibrating TIME across LOBs  
- How much dual-run cost to show in Migrate business cases
