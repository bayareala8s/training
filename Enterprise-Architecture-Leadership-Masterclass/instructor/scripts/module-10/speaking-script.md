# Speaking Script — Module 10: Capstone and Architecture Leadership

**Total runtime target:** 120 minutes  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## [0:00–0:05] Welcome and framing

> Welcome to Module 10: Capstone and Architecture Leadership.  
> Today we turn ten weeks of artifacts into a story executives can fund—and a defense you can stand behind.  
> Presentation format: 15 minutes plus 10 minutes Q&A, maximum 15 slides.

---

## [0:05–0:15] Business scenario

> NorthStar’s committee will not grade your effort. They will decide whether your transformation program is coherent, sequenced, and governable.

**Ask the room:**

> In one sentence, what is NorthStar buying if they say yes?

---

## [0:15–0:35] Narrative construction

> Stakes, diagnosis, destination, journey, guardrails, asks.  
> If a diagram does not set up or discharge a decision, it does not belong in the live deck.

**CFU:** Which three artifacts are load-bearing for your story?

---

## [0:35–0:55] Defending trade-offs

> Use SAY: State criterion, Acknowledge alternative, Yield consequences.  
> We will drill two challenges live.

**Demo challenge:** “Why not multi-cloud now?”

---

## [0:55–1:10] Presentation craft

> Fifteen slides is a feature. Walk the recommended budget.  
> Average about one minute per slide; spend more on asks and roadmap.

---

## [1:10–1:40] Guided finalization lab

> Completeness checklist first. Then story. Then polish.  
> I will triage gaps as I circulate.

**Mid-check:** What is your biggest completeness gap?

---

## [1:40–1:50] Mini dry-run / peer challenge

> Two volunteers: three-minute spine + one SAY defense.

---

## [1:50–2:00] Assignment & panel logistics

> Submit the 24-artifact package, deck, and personal leadership plan.  
> Panel schedule is in BayLearn. Rubric: standard + completeness + presentation criteria.  
> Next: certificate eligibility after thresholds met. Congratulations in advance—for the work, not the slides.

---

## Optional office-hour prompts

- Reconciling contradictory ADRs  
- Cutting a 22-slide deck to 15 without losing the ask
