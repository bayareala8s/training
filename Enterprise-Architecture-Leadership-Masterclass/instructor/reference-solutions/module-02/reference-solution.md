## 3. Sample value-stream friction → investment themes

| Friction | Capability | Theme |
| -------- | ---------- | ----- |
| Conflicting customer IDs across acquisitions | Enterprise Data Management / Identity | Customer golden record + identity federation |
| Manual KYC evidence collection | Risk & Compliance / Partner Management | Straight-through due diligence integrations |
| Product config sprawl across brands | Product Lifecycle Management | Controlled product configuration capability |

**Trade-off to model for students:** Fund golden record before a new marketing channel; otherwise CX “speed” increases defect cost.

## 4. Sample heatmap narrative (excerpt)

- **Customer Onboarding:** Maturity 2/5 — evidence: long cycle time, high rework (case study problems). Confidence: Medium.  
- **Identity & Access:** Maturity 2/5 — inconsistent controls (case study). Confidence: Medium-High.  
- **Integration Platform Management:** Maturity 2/5 — multiple file-transfer platforms. Confidence: High.  
- **Payment Processing:** Maturity 3/5 — works but duplicated across acquisitions. Confidence: Medium.  
- **Workforce Management:** Maturity 4/5 — commodity; not a Year-1 architecture focus. Confidence: Medium.

**Top 3 investments (example):** (1) Customer data/identity integrity for onboarding, (2) Integration platform consolidation, (3) Shared identity as enterprise capability.

## 5. What “excellent” looks like

- Explicit assumptions (e.g., Retail onboarding is the lighthouse; Cards coexistence remains)
- Dual clarity: strategic importance vs. maturity (even if one diagram)
- Stakeholders who benefit and who resist named
- No application disposition lists (that is Module 03)
