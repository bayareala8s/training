**Classification:** Instructor-only  
**Fiction notice:** NorthStar is fictional.

## One-sentence promise

Approve Wave 1 of NorthStar’s enterprise transformation to reduce dual-platform run cost and onboarding cycle time by standardizing landing zones, integration, identity, and governed AI—without a big-bang rewrite.

## Sample decision asks

1. Approve architecture principles and ARB charter.  
2. Fund Wave 1 platform landing-zone and integration backbone expansion.  
3. Adopt TIME dispositions for the top 20 high-risk applications.  
4. Approve AI governance model for incident decision-assist use cases.  
5. Accept 24-month roadmap with quarterly value checkpoints.

## Trade-offs to defend

- Standardization vs. BU autonomy (guardrails + exceptions)  
- Sequence platform before widespread app migration  
- Reject uncontrolled multi-cloud expansion  
- AI with HITL vs. fully automated decisions
