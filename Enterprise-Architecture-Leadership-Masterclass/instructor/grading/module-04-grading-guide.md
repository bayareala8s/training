---

## Quick scoring anchors

### Excellent (4) signals

- Non-goals and constraints present  
- Retain + consolidate + retire all appear thoughtfully  
- Three transitions with **observable** exit criteria  
- Dependencies prevent “all Month 1”  
- Value and risk reduced stated per phase  
- Dual-run treated as managed cost  

### Developing (2) signals

- Vision without dispositions  
- Transitions = project phases only  
- Replace-only bias  
- Vague value (“modernize”)  
- No identity/audit during dual-write  

### Incomplete (1) signals

- Missing transitions or roadmap  
- Generic non-NorthStar content  
- Placeholder text
