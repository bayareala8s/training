## Quick score anchors

### Excellent (4) signals

- Single Accountable per decision class  
- Principles pass quality test; exceptions time-boxed  
- Rejects “ARB for everything” or justifies heavy governance with eyes open  
- Influence plan does not rely on title alone  
- At least two architecture-*function* risks (adoption/politics/capacity)

### Developing (2) signals

- Technology shopping-list principles  
- Org chart only  
- Dual Accountables  
- Security entirely absent  
- “Best practice” claims with no NorthStar fit factors

### Incomplete (1) signals

- Missing majority of deliverables  
- Generic EA essay unrelated to NorthStar  
- Copied framework dump with no decisions

## Feedback bank (portfolio-oriented)

**Strengthen:**  
- “Name the option you rejected and the risk you accepted.”  
- “Rewrite principle X so it changes a decision this quarter.”  
- “Add Security as Consulted on material classes; late engagement is already NorthStar’s failure mode.”  
- “Narrow ARB triggers; define what never needs ARB.”  
- “Replace dual A’s on row Y—who escalates?”

**Affirm:**  
- “Clear non-ownership of backlogs—this will age well into the capstone.”  
- “Exception path is usable under delivery pressure—good.”  
- “Coalition with BU architects is explicit—necessary for hybrid.”

## Participation vs. artifact grading

- Live lab incompleteness ≠ automatic fail if assignment completes deliverables  
- ARB-style debrief participation: score preparation and constructive challenge if cohort policy includes participation  
- Do not grade personality self-scores from the workbook

## Integrity

- Flag submissions that paste the instructor reference solution verbatim (if leaked)  
- Allow structural similarity; require NorthStar-specific rationale in student’s own words  
- Fiction notice should appear on student title block
