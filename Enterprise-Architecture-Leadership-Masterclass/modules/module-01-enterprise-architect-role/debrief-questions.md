# Debrief Questions — Module 01: The Enterprise Architect’s Role

**Use:** End-of-lab architecture review (≈15 minutes) and office hours  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Tone:** ARB-style challenge—ideas, not people

---

## Primary debrief set (live)

1. **Mission clarity**  
   In one sentence, what will NorthStar’s architecture function be *accountable for*—and what will it explicitly *not* own?

2. **Structural trade-off**  
   Which operating-model option did you choose for Year 1, what did you reject, and what risk did you knowingly accept?

3. **Decision rights stress test**  
   Walk a Payments “second API gateway” proposal through your decision classes. Where does it land—and who is Accountable?

4. **Principle quality**  
   Pick your strongest and weakest principle. Apply the quality test: does it change a real decision this quarter?

5. **Influence path**  
   If a BU president bypasses your model next month, what is your first move—and what is your escalation threshold?

6. **Security/resilience**  
   Where do Security and resilience appear in principles, RACI, and engagement—without turning EA into a second GRC office?

7. **Adoption risk**  
   Which risk on your architecture-function register is most likely—and what early signal would tell you it is happening?

8. **Capstone readiness**  
   Which artifact from today will still be useful in Week 10 if NorthStar’s strategy emphasis shifts toward cost vs. speed?

---

## Stretch debrief (if time)

9. How would your model change if the CIO mandated full centralization of architect reporting lines in 90 days?  
10. What metric would you put on a CIO dashboard to show EA value without claiming false causality?

---

## Peer-review prompts (pairs)

- “I can find a single Accountable for each decision class: ___.”  
- “A BU architect would see autonomy here: ___.”  
- “I would bypass this process if: ___—so we should redesign: ___.”

---

## Close (instructor)

Ask one volunteer to complete:

> “Architecture leadership at NorthStar succeeds when ___ without needing ___.”
