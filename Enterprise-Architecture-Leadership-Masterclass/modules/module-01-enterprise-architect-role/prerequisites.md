# Prerequisites — Module 01: The Enterprise Architect’s Role

**Module:** 01 — The Enterprise Architect’s Role  
**Audience:** Newly enrolled BayLearn cohort students

---

## Required before the live session

1. **Read the NorthStar case study baseline**  
   [`course-specification/northstar-case-study.md`](../../course-specification/northstar-case-study.md)  
   Focus on: organization profile, current problems, leadership intent, architecture starting conditions, and stakeholder archetypes.

2. **Confirm your role framing**  
   You are the newly appointed **Lead Enterprise Architect** at NorthStar Financial Services (**fictional**). You do not “own” delivery teams. BU architects report into their lines of business unless your operating model proposes otherwise.

3. **Skim student templates you will use in the lab**  
   - [`student/templates/03-architecture-principles.md`](../../student/templates/03-architecture-principles.md)  
   - [`student/templates/05-raci-matrix.md`](../../student/templates/05-raci-matrix.md)

4. **Complete a 10-minute self-inventory (bring notes)**  
   - Where have you influenced architecture outcomes without formal authority?  
   - Where has “architecture” been ignored or bypassed in your experience?  
   - What do executives in *your* context reward: speed, cost, risk reduction, or novelty?

---

## Recommended background (not blockers)

- 5+ years in engineering, architecture, platform, or technical product leadership
- Familiarity with how financial-services or regulated enterprises balance control and delivery speed
- Comfort reading Mermaid diagrams and writing executive-ready markdown

No prior TOGAF certification, AWS certification, or “enterprise architect” job title is required.

## Pre-work submission (optional but recommended)

Bring one paragraph answering:

> “If NorthStar’s architecture function disappeared tomorrow, which business outcomes would get worse first—and why?”

Instructors may cold-call this opening. There is no single correct answer; depth of causal reasoning matters.
