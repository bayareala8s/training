## Description (paste-ready)

Module 01 of the **Enterprise Architecture Leadership Masterclass** (BayLearn | BayAreaLa8s).

**Topic:** The Enterprise Architect’s Role  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (**fictional** instructional enterprise)  
**Student role:** Lead Enterprise Architect

NorthStar already has architects inside business units—but no consistent enterprise governance. Local decisions create duplicate platforms, late security engagement, and weak executive visibility into technical risk.

In this session we cover:

- What enterprise architecture really is (and is not)  
- Centralized vs. federated vs. hybrid operating models—with trade-offs  
- Decision rights, RACI, and engagement modes (consult / collaborate / govern)  
- Architecture principles that constrain decisions without becoming tech shopping lists  
- Leadership assessment: influencing outcomes without hierarchical authority  

**Lab:** Establish NorthStar’s Architecture Function — mission, operating model diagram, RACI, 8–10 principles, decision rights, engagement model, and risk register.
