## Readiness self-check

Answer “yes” to at least four of five before the session:

1. I can name NorthStar’s top strategic themes (cost, CX, speed, risk, AI, visibility).
2. I can explain why coexistence matters for acquired lines of business.
3. I know what TIME dispositions I recommended for at least five applications.
4. I can draft principles that constrain target-state choices (not just slogans).
5. I accept that a roadmap without dependencies is a wish list, not architecture leadership.

If fewer than four, complete the catch-up brief before attempting the lab.
