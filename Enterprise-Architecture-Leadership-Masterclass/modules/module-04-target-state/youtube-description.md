## Description (paste-ready)

In Module 04 of the Enterprise Architecture Leadership Masterclass, you step into the role of Lead Enterprise Architect at NorthStar Financial Services—a fictional financial services enterprise with 300+ applications, acquired lines of business, and pressure to cut operating cost while improving customer onboarding and resilience.

This session covers:

• Defining a defensible target-state architecture (capabilities, principles, constraints, non-goals)  
• Modernization strategies: rehost, replatform, refactor, replace, retire, retain, consolidate  
• Transition architectures that manage coexistence—with observable exit criteria  
• Building a 24-month architecture roadmap sequenced by value, risk reduction, and dependencies  

Lab: Create NorthStar’s Target-State Roadmap—including application dispositions, three transition states, phase value, risks, and an executive summary.
