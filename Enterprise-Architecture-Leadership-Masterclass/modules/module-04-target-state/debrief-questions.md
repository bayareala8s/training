# Debrief Questions — Module 04

**Module:** Target-State Architecture and Transformation Roadmaps  
**Use:** End-of-session debrief (10–15 minutes) and office hours  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## Primary debrief (ask 4–6)

1. **What did you deliberately put in non-goals—and what pressure will make someone reopen them?**
2. **Which disposition was hardest: retain, replace, consolidate, or retire? What evidence would change it?**
3. **Where does coexistence create the most risk at NorthStar—data, identity, payments, or partner integrations?**
4. **Which dependency, if delayed, collapses your Phase 2 value story?**
5. **If the CFO cuts 30% of funding, what outcome do you renegotiate first?**
6. **How will you know Transition A is truly done—not just “projects green”?**

---

## Architecture judgment

7. When is a long dual-run the **correct** risk trade-off versus a faster cutover?
8. How should principles constrain a BU that wants a fourth CRM “just for wealth”?
9. What belongs in the EA roadmap versus the product organization’s delivery plan?

---

## Leadership and influence

10. How do you communicate a consolidate decision to the losing BU without pretending there is no loser?
11. Where should the CISO hold a hard gate in your transition sequence?
12. What personal leadership habit from Module 01 helped (or hurt) your Module 04 artifact quality?

---

## Capstone forward look

13. Which Module 04 artifacts will you reuse unchanged in the capstone—and which will need Module 05–07 detail?
14. What open question must Module 05 (cloud/platform) answer before your foundation phase is credible?

---

## Optional pair share (2 minutes each)

- One trade-off you are proud of defending  
- One assumption you made that still worries you
