# Common Mistakes — Module 02

**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## Mistakes to avoid

1. **System-named capabilities** — “MainframePaymentsCapability” is an application label, not a capability.
2. **Org-chart cloning** — Mapping LOBs as capabilities recreates silos instead of exposing shared needs.
3. **Everything is Core** — If nothing is commodity, you cannot standardize or buy wisely.
4. **Heatmap without evidence** — Red/amber/green based on anecdotes fails in ExCo challenge.
5. **Value stream as sequence diagram** — Listing systems in call order is not value-stream analysis.
6. **Ignoring risk stages** — Financial-services onboarding without KYC/monitoring stages is incomplete.
7. **Ten Priority-1 investments** — Dilutes leadership attention; pick a sharp set.
8. **Architecture owns business capabilities** — EA facilitates; business executives remain accountable owners.
9. **Skipping assumptions** — Post-acquisition NorthStar data is messy; state what you assumed.
10. **Jumping to Module 03 solutions** — Do not disposition applications before the capability frame exists.
