# Prerequisites — Module 02

**Module:** Business Architecture and Capability Mapping  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## Required

- Completion of Module 01 concepts: EA role, operating model basics, decision rights awareness
- Familiarity with NorthStar case study baseline: [`../../../course-specification/northstar-case-study.md`](../../../course-specification/northstar-case-study.md)
- Ability to read executive strategy themes and translate them into questions (not yet into designs)

---

## Recommended preparation (30–45 minutes)

1. Re-read NorthStar strategic themes and named value streams in the case study.
2. Skim templates:
   - [`../../../student/templates/06-capability-map.md`](../../../student/templates/06-capability-map.md)
   - [`../../../student/templates/21-value-stream-map.md`](../../../student/templates/21-value-stream-map.md)
   - [`../../../student/templates/22-capability-heatmap.md`](../../../student/templates/22-capability-heatmap.md)
3. Bring one example from your workplace (or prior experience) of a “capability” that was really a system or a team—be ready to discuss why that confusion is costly.

---

## Assumed knowledge

| Topic | Level expected |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Strategy vs. roadmap vs. project | Conceptual |
| Org charts vs. operating models | Conceptual |
| Basic financial-services domains (customer, payments, risk) | Working vocabulary |
| Mermaid or simple diagramming | Optional but helpful |

---

## Not required

- Prior business architecture certification (TOGAF/BIZBOK familiarity helpful but not required)
- Detailed knowledge of NorthStar’s fictional application estate (that arrives in Module 03)
- Cloud or AWS skills
