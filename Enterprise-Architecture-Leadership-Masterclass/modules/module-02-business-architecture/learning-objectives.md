## Success criteria (observable)

A proficient student submission:

- Distinguishes **capabilities** (what the business does) from **applications**, **org units**, and **projects**
- Covers ~8–15 Level 1 capabilities relevant to NorthStar (payments, customer, partner, risk, platforms, etc.)
- Expands at least five Level 1 capabilities to Level 2
- Marks Core / Supporting / Commodity with a short rationale
- Includes ownership that is plausible for a multi-LOB financial enterprise
- Shows at least one value stream (Customer Onboarding recommended) with stage → capability mapping
- Heatmap colors are backed by maturity evidence, not aesthetic preference
- Explicitly names 2–3 investment themes tied to NorthStar strategy (cost, CX, speed, resilience)

---

## Leadership behaviors emphasized

- Facilitating capability workshops without letting the loudest system owner redefine “capability”
- Saying “not yet” to solution debates until the capability and outcome frame is clear
- Presenting heatmaps as **decision aids**, not as absolute truth
