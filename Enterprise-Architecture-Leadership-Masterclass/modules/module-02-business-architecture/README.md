# Module 02 — Business Architecture and Capability Mapping

**Week:** 2  
**Duration:** 2-hour live session + lab/assignment  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Student role:** Lead Enterprise Architect

---

## Module objective

Translate NorthStar’s business strategy into a usable business capability map, value-stream views, and investment heatmaps that guide architecture priorities—independent of org charts and current systems.

---

## Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

1. Translate strategy themes into architecture priorities, constraints, and investment questions.
2. Build Level 1–2 capability maps with ownership, type (core/supporting/commodity), and maturity.
3. Map value streams to capabilities and identify friction points that architecture can address.
4. Connect stakeholders, outcomes/KPIs, and capability gaps into an executive-ready heatmap narrative.

Full detail: [`learning-objectives.md`](learning-objectives.md)
