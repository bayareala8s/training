# Common Mistakes — Module 09

1. **Treating ARB as a veto theater** — Rejecting without offering a feasible path that meets business timing.
2. **Approve-with-conditions without owners** — Conditions that nobody can verify become silent approvals.
3. **One mega-ADR for four independent decisions** — Cloud, database, integration framework, and prod access deserve separate decision records.
4. **Jargon-first memos** — Executives need cost, risk, customer impact, and the ask—not technology catalogs.
5. **Permanent exceptions** — Exceptions without expiry, compensating controls, and remediation plans become the new standard.
6. **Skipping role fidelity** — Everyone arguing as “generic architect” collapses the simulation’s learning value.
7. **Focusing on slide aesthetics** — Governance quality is disposition quality, not diagram polish.
8. **Ignoring precedent** — Approving a second cloud “just once” invites every BU to ask next quarter.
