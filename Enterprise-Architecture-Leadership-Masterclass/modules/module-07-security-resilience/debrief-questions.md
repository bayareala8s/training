# Debrief Questions — Module 07

Use after the guided lab (≈15 minutes). Prefer volunteer artifacts over lecture.

---

## Architecture

1. Where did your trust boundaries change, and which control enforced each change?
2. Which STRIDE risk was hardest to control cheaply—and what did you accept as residual?
3. If NorthStar funded only one uplift next quarter (IAM hardening, CRR, or recovery drills), which would you choose and why?

---

## Operations and cost

4. What did your failure/recovery test prove that configuration screenshots alone could not?
5. How would overnight CRR + versioning change your cost envelope versus a quarterly DR drill?

---

## Leadership and compliance

6. Walk the room through one row of your control-evidence matrix as if speaking to internal audit.
7. How would you time-box an exception for a business unit that “needs” broad S3 list permissions?

---

## Capstone bridge

8. Which Module 07 artifacts will you reuse unchanged in the capstone, and which will you harden?
