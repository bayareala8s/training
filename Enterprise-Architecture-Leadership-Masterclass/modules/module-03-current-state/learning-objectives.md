## Success criteria (observable)

A proficient student submission:

- Scopes assessment (e.g., by capability, criticality, or cost) rather than scoring all 300+ imagined apps equally
- Uses the fictional CSV as evidence, not gospel—dispositions are challenged with rationale
- Connects TIME outcomes back to Module 02 capabilities and strategy themes
- Shows at least one dependency hub and one duplicate-capability cluster
- Top-10 risks include business impact, likelihood/exposure, and recommended response
- Avoids “replace everything” narratives; respects coexistence constraints
