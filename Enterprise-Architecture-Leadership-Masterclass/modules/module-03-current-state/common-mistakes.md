# Common Mistakes — Module 03

1. **Boiling the ocean** — Scoring every imagined app instead of scoping.
2. **Blindly copying CSV dispositions** — Seeds are for challenge, not copy-paste.
3. **TIME without capability successor** — Eliminate with nowhere for the capability to land.
4. **Ignoring hubs** — Migrating spokes while the ESB remains unspoken.
5. **Tech-only debt lists** — No business impact language for executives.
6. **Ten unrelated Priority-1 risks** — No narrative coherence.
7. **Treating Tolerate as failure** — Sometimes Tolerate is the correct leadership call.
8. **Forgetting coexistence cost** — Dual-run is real OpEx during Migrate.
9. **Disconnect from Module 02** — Portfolio work that ignores capability themes.
10. **Presenting fiction as fact externally** — Always keep the fiction notice in portfolio samples.
