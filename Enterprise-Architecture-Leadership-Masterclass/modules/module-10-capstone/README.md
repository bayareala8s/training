# Module 10 — Capstone and Architecture Leadership

**Week:** 10  
**Duration:** 2-hour live session + capstone finalization and panel presentation  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Student role:** Lead Enterprise Architect  
**Slug:** `module-10-capstone`

---

## Module objective

Integrate the NorthStar Enterprise Transformation Program into a coherent executive narrative, defend architecture trade-offs under panel scrutiny, deliver a 15-minute executive presentation (max 15 slides) plus 10-minute Q&A, and close with a personal architecture leadership career plan.

---

## Learning objectives

Students will be able to:

1. Synthesize multi-module artifacts into a single transformation narrative with clear decision asks.
2. Defend architecture trade-offs under executive and peer challenge.
3. Deliver an executive presentation within time and slide constraints.
4. Build a personal leadership and portfolio plan for the next 12–24 months.

Full detail: [`learning-objectives.md`](learning-objectives.md)
