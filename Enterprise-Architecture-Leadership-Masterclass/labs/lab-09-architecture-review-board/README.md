# Lab 09 — Conduct an Architecture Review Board

**Module:** 09 — Architecture Governance and Executive Communication  
**Estimated duration:** 90–120 minutes (40 minutes in-session + completion async)  
**Estimated cost:** N/A (non-AWS)  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)  
**Student role:** Rotating ARB roles; submission as Lead EA package

---

## Lab title

Conduct an Architecture Review Board on Retail Payments’ divergent modernization proposal.

## Business context

Retail Payments proposes a “fast path” that conflicts with NorthStar’s emerging enterprise standards. The CIO requires an ARB disposition before work proceeds. Students review the proposal pack, simulate the board, and produce decision artifacts.

## Learning objectives

1. Apply risk-tiered architecture review criteria to a multi-issue proposal.
2. Practice role-based constructive challenge and disposition language.
3. Produce executive-ready decision records (memo + ADRs).
