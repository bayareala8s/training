# Stretch Objectives — Lab 01: Architecture Operating Model

**Status:** Optional — do **not** sacrifice core deliverables  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## S1 — Stakeholder influence map

Using [`student/templates/04-stakeholder-matrix.md`](../../student/templates/04-stakeholder-matrix.md), map CIO, CISO, one BU president, Platform lead, and two BU architects on interest/influence. Annotate your first 30-day conversation plan.

## S2 — ARB one-pager (preview of Module 09)

Draft a one-page ARB charter: purpose, membership, cadence, inputs, outputs, and decision types **in scope / out of scope**. Keep it narrow for Year 1.

## S3 — Metrics dashboard sketch

Propose 5 CIO-facing leading indicators for EA value (e.g., % services on golden paths, exception aging, ADR coverage on material decisions). Note false-causality risks for each metric.

## S4 — Contra-model memo

Write 400–600 words arguing the *opposite* of your chosen structural option as if you were a skeptical BU president—then rebut yourself in 200 words. Strengthens trade-off muscle.

## S5 — Personal leadership delta

Complete the self-score table from Lesson 1.4 and draft a 90-day practice plan for your two lowest scores (feeds later personal leadership plan template).
