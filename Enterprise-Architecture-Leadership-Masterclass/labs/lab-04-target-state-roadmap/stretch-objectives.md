# Lab 04 — Stretch Objectives

**Lab:** Create NorthStar’s Target-State Roadmap  
**Optional:** Does not replace required deliverables  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

---

## Stretch 1 — Wave capacity model

Estimate delivery capacity constraints (platform team, integration team, BU change windows). Show which roadmap items cannot start in parallel and why.

## Stretch 2 — Dual-run cost burn-down

Create a simple 24-month sketch of dual-run cost (qualitative 1–5 or illustrative $ bands) for CRM and file-bridge coexistence. Tie burn-down to Transition C exit criteria.

## Stretch 3 — ADR pack

Write two ADRs:

1. CRM survivor / consolidate decision  
2. Partner integration platform approach (API/event vs file survivor)

## Stretch 4 — Regulatory evidence in transitions

Add a one-page note: what audit evidence exists in Transition B for customer data dual-write and identity controls (control classes only—do not invent fake regulator filings).

## Stretch 5 — Executive challenge memo

Write a one-page response anticipating CFO and CISO objections to your Phase 2 plan; revise sequencing if needed.

## Stretch 6 — Mermaid pack

Produce a polished diagram set: target patterns, coexistence, and Gantt/timeline for the roadmap suitable for slide insertion.
