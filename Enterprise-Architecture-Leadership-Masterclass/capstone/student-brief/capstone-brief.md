# Capstone Brief — NorthStar Enterprise Transformation Program

**Course:** Enterprise Architecture Leadership Masterclass  
**Platform:** BayLearn  
**Student role:** Lead Enterprise Architect, NorthStar Financial Services *(fictional)*  
**Format:** Formal architecture review  

---

## Presentation format

| Constraint | Requirement |
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Presentation length | **15 minutes** |
| Q&A | **10 minutes** |
| Maximum slides | **15** |

---

## Objective

Integrate your weekly artifacts into one coherent enterprise transformation proposal that an executive committee could decide on.

### Notes

- Artifact **22** requires **five distinct ADRs** covering cloud/platform, integration, identity/access, master data, and governed AI themes (titles may vary with instructor approval if breadth is preserved).
- Artifact **24** is the live presentation deck (≤15 slides).
- Personal leadership plan is required for the Module 10 assignment companion but is **not** one of the 24 capstone document artifacts.
- You may refine earlier weekly submissions; do not start from blank if prior labs are complete.

## Presentation storyline (recommended)

1. Business problem and outcomes  
2. Current-state reality (portfolio + risk)  
3. Target-state vision  
4. Roadmap and value by phase  
5. Platform / integration / security / AI highlights  
6. Governance model  
7. Explicit decisions requested  
8. Risks and asks
