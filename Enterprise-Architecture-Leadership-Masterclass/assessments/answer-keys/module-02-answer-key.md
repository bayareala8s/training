## Scenario guidance

**S1:** Strong answers prioritize customer identity/data integrity and/or KYC straight-through capability on the critical path; treat new channel as secondary. Alternatives: channel-first (speed theater) or big-bang onboarding rewrite (feasibility risk).

**S2:** Either choice can be defended. Excellent answers cite ExCo themes, regulated identity risk, integration run-cost, residual risk, and a sequencing bridge (e.g., tactical controls while the other waits). Weak answers ignore residual risk.

**S3:** Facilitation: ban app names; rewrite as business capabilities; classify types; assign business owners; park inventory for Module 03. Success: ≥8 L1 independent names + owners + types.

## Discussion facilitation notes

**D1:** Allow dual-parent or primary/secondary ownership; document assumption; avoid endless taxonomy wars.

**D2:** Broad maps fit multi-LOB NorthStar; narrow maps fit pilots. Cost of broad is facilitation time; cost of narrow is blind spots.
