## Scenario guidance

**S1:** Prefer reject second cloud; accelerate landing zone; phase merchant onboarding before settlement complexity; name schedule residual risk.  
**S2:** Strong reject or non-prod-only evaluation; cite keys, PITR, lock-in, golden record—not throughput alone.  
**S3:** Reject framework standardization; salvage by translating mappings to enterprise patterns; stop new DSL investment; timebox rewrite.

## Discussion cues

**D1:** Offer fast-path enablement, shared outcomes, transparent criteria, no public humiliation.  
**D2:** Sovereignty, acquisition constraints, or regulator-driven residency—with unified identity/logging and explicit exception expiry.
