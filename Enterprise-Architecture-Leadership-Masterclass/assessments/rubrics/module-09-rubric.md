# Module 09 Rubric Notes

Inherits [`standard-architecture-rubric.md`](standard-architecture-rubric.md).

## Emphasis overrides for scoring conversations

- Weight discussion toward **trade-off analysis**, **security and resilience**, and **communication quality**.  
- Participation scored separately for ARB simulation.

## Module-specific Excellent (4) signals

- Four requests disposed independently with precedent awareness  
- Alternate path is sequenced and owned  
- Memo lead sentence is a clear ask  
- ADRs include real non-chosen options and negative consequences  
- Access model is least-privilege and time-bound  

## Module-specific Incomplete (1) signals

- Blanket approve due to funding/date  
- No security treatment of contractor access  
- No ADR alternatives
