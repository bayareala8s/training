# Module 04 Rubric Notes

**Module:** Target-State Architecture and Transformation Roadmaps  
**Base rubric:** [`standard-architecture-rubric.md`](standard-architecture-rubric.md)  
**Grading guide:** `instructor/grading/module-04-grading-guide.md`  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

Module 04 uses the **standard architecture rubric weights** without override. These notes clarify evidence expected for this module’s artifacts.

---

## Evidence by criterion

### Business alignment (20%)

**Look for:** Strategic capabilities linked to cost/CX/speed/risk themes; phase value stated as outcomes; executive ask clear.  
**Downgrade if:** Technology fashion (AI/cloud) without outcome trace; generic industry boilerplate.

### Architecture quality (20%)

**Look for:** Pattern-level target; SoR vs SoE; shared platforms; non-goals; coherent disposition table.  
**Downgrade if:** Instance laundry list; contradictory patterns; no ownership implications.

### Trade-off analysis (20%)

**Look for:** Alternatives for at least key decisions (CRM, StarCore, file hubs); consequences across cost/risk/speed.  
**Downgrade if:** Single “best practice” path; replace-everything without dual-run economics.

### Security and resilience (15%)

**Look for:** Identity, audit evidence, and reconciliation during dual-write/coexistence; RTO/RPO class mentions for critical journeys.  
**Downgrade if:** Entirely silent on controls during transitions.  
**Note:** Depth expected is proportionate—Module 07 goes deeper; negligence still penalized.

### Feasibility and roadmap (15%)

**Look for:** Three transitions with observable exits; 24-month waves; dependencies; dual-run burn-down thinking; capacity realism.  
**Downgrade if:** All initiatives Month 1; transitions without exits; big-bang core rewrite.

### Communication quality (10%)

**Look for:** Exec summary ≤1 page; scannable tables; diagrams that support narrative.  
**Downgrade if:** Jargon-first; incomplete sections; placeholders.

---

## Module-specific minimum bar (pass guidance)

To reach overall Proficient (~3 average), submissions typically must include:

1. Principles + non-goals  
2. ≥8 dispositions including retain, consolidate, and retire  
3. Transitions A/B/C with exits  
4. Roadmap with dependencies and value per phase  
5. Risks + executive summary  

Missing any of (3) or (4) usually caps Feasibility at ≤2.
