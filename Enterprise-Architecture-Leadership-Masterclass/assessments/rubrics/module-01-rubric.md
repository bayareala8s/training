# Rubric Notes — Module 01: The Enterprise Architect’s Role

**Base rubric:** [`standard-architecture-rubric.md`](standard-architecture-rubric.md)  
**Weights:** Unchanged (use standard weights)  
**Case study:** NorthStar Financial Services (fictional)

Module 01 does **not** override criterion weights. These notes calibrate scoring for an early, non-AWS operating-model module.

---

## Emphasis summary

| Criterion | Weight | Module 01 calibration |
| --------- | -----: | --------------------- |
| Business alignment | 20% | **Emphasize.** Mission and principles must tie to NorthStar intents (cost, speed, resilience, platforms, governed AI, visibility). |
| Architecture quality | 20% | **Emphasize.** Decision classes, single Accountable RACI, coherent diagram, engagement clarity. |
| Trade-off analysis | 20% | **Emphasize.** Structural option + rejected alternatives; ARB scope rationale; no “best practice” dogma. |
| Security and resilience | 15% | **Proportionate.** Do not require threat models or RTO worksheets. Still penalize total omission of Security/resilience in principles or RACI. |
| Feasibility and roadmap | 15% | **Year-1 realism.** Credible hybrid/central/federate choice; usable SLAs; 90-day influence plan. Not a 3-year Gantt. |
| Communication quality | 10% | **Emphasize.** CIO-skimmable; plain language; fiction notice on title block. |

---

## What “Excellent (4)” looks like this week

- Clear non-ownership of delivery backlogs (unless explicitly justified)  
- Hybrid or alternative defended with NorthStar fit factors  
- 8–10 principles with exceptions and signals; not vendor lists  
- Narrow govern mode; guardrails preferred  
- Influence plan uses multiple sources beyond title  
- Architecture-function risks included (bypass, capacity, adoption)

## What triggers Developing (2) or lower

- Shopping-list principles  
- Org chart without decision rights  
- Dual Accountables  
- ARB-for-everything without acknowledging bypass risk  
- Generic EA essay with no NorthStar decisions  
- Security/resilience entirely absent
