## Scenario questions (3)

### S1 — [Application] [LO: MLO-2.3] [Difficulty: Medium]

**Scenario:** NorthStar’s median time-to-activate a retail customer is far above peers. Product wants a new mobile intake channel in 90 days. Risk reports manual KYC evidence gathering. Data reports duplicate customer keys across two acquisitions.

**Prompt:** Recommend the first capability uplift and explain how it connects to the onboarding value stream. Name one alternative you are not choosing and why.

**Response guidance (student-facing):** 150–250 words; state recommendation, alternatives, and primary risk.

### S2 — [Trade-off analysis] [LO: MLO-2.4] [Difficulty: Hard]

**Scenario:** Your heatmap shows Integration Platform Management and Identity & Access both deep red. Budget allows a major bet on only one this quarter. Payments LOB wants integration consolidation; CISO wants identity.

**Prompt:** Choose one, justify using strategy themes and risk, and describe the residual risk of the deferred bet.

### S3 — [Architecture judgment] [LO: MLO-2.2] [Difficulty: Hard]

**Scenario:** A Cards LOB architect submits a “capability map” that is a redraw of their application inventory grouped by team.

**Prompt:** How do you coach them to remediate the artifact in one working session? List the facilitation steps and success criteria.
