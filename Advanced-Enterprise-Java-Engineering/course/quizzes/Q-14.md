<summary>Spoiler — answers</summary>

1. **B.** Edge TLS vs a healthy process.
2. **B.** Execution role ≠ task role.
3. **B.** KMS-backed secrets, never git.
4. **B.** ~52 minutes; region is DR, not free 99.99%.
5. **B.** RPO = loss; RTO = time to serve again.
6. **B.** Do not fail over to the cell.
7. **B.** Unused failover / DR headroom.
8. **B.** Threat notes, not exploit scripts.
