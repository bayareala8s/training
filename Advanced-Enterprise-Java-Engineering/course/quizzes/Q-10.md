<details>
<summary>Spoiler — answers</summary>

1. **B.** Controllers restore replicas.
2. **B.** Same L7 job, different objects.
3. **B.** Not Ready or bad selector → no Endpoints.
4. **B.** Kill vs traffic are different questions.
5. **B.** cgroup OOM; never `Xmx` = limit.
6. **B.** Key names are the contract.
7. **B.** Label mismatch.
8. **B.** Paper + YAML; no licensed cluster.
