# Weekly Study Routine

A structured weekly rhythm balances theory, practice, production study, and interview performance.

## Recommended Schedule

### Monday — Theory

Read one chapter or major section. Take notes using the explanation pattern: definition, problem, intuition, mechanism, guarantees, failures, tradeoffs.

### Tuesday — Algorithms and Coding (if applicable)

Work through mechanisms step by step. For principal loops: prioritize [design-adjacent problems](/docs/coding-preparation/design-adjacent-problems) over LeetCode grind. Draw sequence diagrams, state machines, and failure-domain diagrams. Explain aloud without notes.

### Wednesday — Production Implementations

Study case studies in `case-studies/`. Read engineering blogs and papers referenced in chapters. Compare theory to production choices.

### Thursday — Hands-On Lab

Complete one lab from `labs/` or implement a small algorithm (consistent hashing, vector clocks, rate limiter).

### Friday — Interview Questions

Answer 5–10 principal-level questions aloud. Record yourself. Compare to strong-answer outlines in chapters.

### Saturday — System Design

Complete one 60-minute system-design exercise. Use the template in `templates/system-design-template.md`.

### Sunday — Review and Reflect

- Review flashcards
- Update `progress/weak-areas.yaml`
- Run `python3 scripts/generate_progress_report.py`
- Record a 10-minute whiteboard explanation of the week's topic

## Tips

- **Explain aloud** — interview performance requires verbal fluency, not just reading comprehension
- **Time-box exercises** — principal interviews are timed; practice under pressure
- **Update progress metadata** — tracking weak areas directs study efficiently
- **Connect to experience** — map every topic to something you have built or operated
