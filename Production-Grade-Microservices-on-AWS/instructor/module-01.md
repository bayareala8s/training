## Key Teaching Points

- Microservices solve **organizational** scale problems as much as technical ones
- Bounded contexts prevent “nanoservices” if sized to business capabilities
- Emphasize **data ownership** early—it pays off in Module 6

## Lab Facilitation

- Circulate during context mapping; challenge shared-database designs
- Good context maps show **events** between Orders and Notifications

## Common Mistakes

- Too many services (8+ for capstone scope)
- No clear event boundaries
